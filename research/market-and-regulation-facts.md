## Noise complaints and closures — HONESTY RULE

No verified nationwide percentage of German venues facing noise complaints, and no verified national noise-closure count, was found. Decks must frame noise as **documented displacement risk** (resident complaints, arrival/departure and smoking-area noise, land-use conflicts — LiveKomm "Red List", Live-DMA/MIZ material), never as a fake national statistic. A LiveKomm Red List exists (20 of 21 listed venues in large cities, undated, via miz.org).

## TA Lärm (German noise protection)

Nationwide administrative framework for commercial noise; clubs assessed as entertainment venues. Commonly cited immission limits at the protected location (verify against official TA Lärm Table 1 before any external use):

| Area category | Day 06–22 | Night 22–06 |
|---|---|---|
| General residential | 55 dB(A) | 40 dB(A) |
| Urban/mixed core | 60 dB(A) | 45 dB(A) |
| Pure residential | 50 dB(A) | 35 dB(A) |

Limits are at the receptor, not inside the club. Sperrzeit (closing hours) is jurisdiction-specific (state/municipal; Gaststättenverordnung) — never quote a national closing time.
